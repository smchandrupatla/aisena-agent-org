Control Plane

Autonomy with explicit risk boundaries.

Technical decisions are autonomous; business-impacting decisions route through human approval gates.

Approval Gate Triggers

Trigger Why escalation is mandatory
Production system change Can affect live users and service continuity
Real infrastructure or API spend Introduces financial commitment
User-data handling changes Privacy, legal, and trust exposure
Pricing or legal impact Requires business-level risk judgment

Conflict Arbitration

  1. Level 1
    Direct trade-off analysis Roles document alternatives and expected impact.
  2. Level 2
    Architecture arbitration Solution Architect resolves technical conflict.
  3. Level 3
    Policy arbitration Security and compliance role enforces policy constraints.
  4. Level 4
    Business decision Product Owner decides risk tolerance trade-offs.

Release Safety Checklist

Every release candidate must include version pinning, rollback steps, and quality evidence.
Commit reference linked Rollback command tested Critical tests green Security scan reviewed Change log appended
If a requested action falls outside technical judgment and into business risk ownership, escalation is required before execution.
