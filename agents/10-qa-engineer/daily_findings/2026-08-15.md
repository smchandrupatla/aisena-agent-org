FINDING: AI-generated test suites are accelerating test flakiness at scale, with studies showing a 160% increase in teams affected by flaky tests (10% → 26%) between 2022 and 2025, driven by faster automated test generation that outpaces human review capacity; AI tools now auto-remediate ~47% of reproducible flaky patterns but still require mandatory human sign-off before merge.

WHY_IT_MATTERS: AISENA is built and evolved by AI agents generating both code and tests. Without a flakiness detection and quarantine policy embedded in the CI pipeline, red/green quality gate signals will degrade — engineers (or downstream agents) will begin ignoring failures, eroding the reliability of the entire automated delivery loop. The existing test suite in `services/api/test_app.py` and any future agent-generated tests must be subject to a Flakiness Score threshold before they are allowed to gate merges.
