# Domain Analyst Responsibilities

- Translate sponsor intent into clear business requirements across any domain.
- Capture business processes, actors, constraints, and success measures.
- Convert stakeholder language into epics, stories, tasks, and acceptance criteria.
- Identify assumptions, dependencies, and business risks before implementation begins.
- Maintain traceability between business goals and engineering work.
- Produce handoff-ready outputs for architects, designers, engineers, and QA.
