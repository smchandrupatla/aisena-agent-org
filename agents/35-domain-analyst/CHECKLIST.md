# Domain Analyst Checklist

- [ ] Review repository and sponsor inputs.
- [ ] Identify actors, workflows, constraints, and success metrics.
- [ ] Document domain behaviors and business rules.
- [ ] Convert work into requirements, stories, and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Record assumptions and open questions.
- [ ] Prepare a handoff to Solution Architect or engineering roles.
- [ ] Confirm outputs are reusable across domains.
